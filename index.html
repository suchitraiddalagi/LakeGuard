WATER QUALITY INDICATORS

What Do We Monitor?

These four parameters are used in our demonstration dashboard to explain different aspects of water quality.

🧪

pH Level

Indicates whether water is acidic, neutral, or alkaline.

🌫️

Turbidity

Indicates how cloudy water is due to suspended particles.

🐟

Dissolved Oxygen

Measures oxygen dissolved in water, which is important for aquatic life.

🌡️

Water Temperature

Shows the water temperature, which can affect aquatic organisms and oxygen levels.
